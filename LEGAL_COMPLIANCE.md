## 🔍 Data Sources Verification

### Primary Data Sources
1. **NIST National Vulnerability Database (NVD)**
   - URL: https://nvd.nist.gov/
   - Public API: https://services.nvd.nist.gov/rest/json/cves/1.0
   - Classification: **Official US Government Data**

2. **CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities**
   - URL: https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog
   - Official Feed: Known Exploited Vulnerabilities JSON
   - Classification: **Official US Government Data**

3. **Public Threat Intelligence**
   - Shodan (public internet scan aggregation)
   - Censys (certificate transparency aggregation)
   - OSINT feeds (open-source intelligence)
   - Security research publications

### Data Characteristics
- ✅ **Publicly available** - All data is already disclosed to the public
- ✅ **Aggregated** - Not targeting specific victims or organizations
- ✅ **Geolocation anonymized** - Regional distribution, not exact coordinates
